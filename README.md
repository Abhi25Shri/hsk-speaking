## 🎤 HSK 1 Speaking Practice

An interactive web-based application for practicing HSK (Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi) Level 1 Chinese speaking skills.

### Features

- **📚 Vocabulary Practice**: Learn 150+ HSK 1 essential words with Chinese characters, pinyin, and English translations
- **🔊 Audio Pronunciation**: Built-in text-to-speech to help with proper pronunciation
- **🎴 Flashcard Learning**: Interactive flashcard mode with flip animation for review
- **💬 Dialogue Practice**: Real-world dialogue scenarios including greetings, introductions, and polite phrases
- **🔍 Search & Filter**: Search words by Chinese, pinyin, or English, and filter by category
- **📊 Progress Tracking**: Monitor your learning progress with session statistics

### Vocabulary Categories

- **Greetings**: Hello, thank you, goodbye, sorry
- **Numbers**: 1-10 and related counting
- **Family**: Family members and relationships
- **Food**: Common food and beverage items
- **Actions**: Common verbs and actions

### How to Use

1. **Vocabulary Tab**: 
   - Browse all HSK 1 words organized by category
   - Use the search box to find specific words
   - Click the 🔊 button to hear pronunciation
   - Filter by category to focus on specific topics

2. **Flashcards Tab**:
   - Review words using interactive flashcards
   - Hover over or click to flip the card
   - Use arrow buttons to navigate between cards
   - Click 🔊 to hear pronunciation

3. **Dialogues Tab**:
   - Select from preset dialogue scenarios
   - Practice real-world Chinese conversations
   - Hear each line pronounced with the 🔊 button
   - Learn context and proper usage

### Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Arrow Right**: Next flashcard
- **Arrow Left**: Previous flashcard
- **Spacebar**: Speak current word
